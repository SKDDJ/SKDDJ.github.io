The student visa is pending; the doctorate will be on data agents and memory.

Research intern at [TSAIL](https://ml.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/), Tsinghua University (July 2024–September 2025), with Prof. Jun Zhu, Dr. Zehua Chen, and Dr. Duo Su.
