## Research

My current work is on data agents and memory: how a system keeps, revises, and uses experience over a long horizon, including personalization and lifelong learning.

I have also worked on post-training and multimodal models, including parameter-efficient fine-tuning and diffusion-based adaptation, and on dataset distillation. A separate interest is abstract reasoning, including tasks in the ARC-AGI setting.
