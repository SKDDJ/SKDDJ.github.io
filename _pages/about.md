Yiming Shi (史一鸣) is an Ace Top Talent Intern at [Dots](https://studio.dots.ai/) (Xiaohongshu), working on abstract reasoning for ARC-AGI-2 and ARC-AGI-3. He is an incoming Ph.D. student in computer science at The University of Queensland, co-advised by Prof. Zhifeng Bao and Dr. Hai Lan, with his doctoral research on data agents and memory.

He was a research intern in multimodal understanding at [Zhipu AI](https://z.ai) from March to August 2025, and a research intern at [TSAIL](https://ml.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/), Tsinghua University, from July 2024 to September 2025, working with Prof. Jun Zhu, Dr. Zehua Chen, and Dr. Duo Su. From May 2023 to July 2024 he was a research intern at the [Center for Future Media](https://cfm.uestc.edu.cn/index), UESTC, with Prof. Yang Yang and Dr. Jiwei Wei. From November 2022 to May 2023 he worked on DevOps with [Star Studio](https://github.com/StarStudio) at UESTC. He received a B.Sc. in Computer Science and a B.Ec. in Finance, Internet+ Dual Degree, from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in June 2025.
