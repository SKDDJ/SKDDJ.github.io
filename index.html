Yiming Shi

Ace Top Talent Intern @ Dots · Incoming PhD @ UQ

Beijing, China

Yiming Shi (史一鸣) is an Ace Top Talent Intern at Dots (Xiaohongshu), on abstract reasoning for ARC-AGI-2 and ARC-AGI-3. Incoming Ph.D. student in computer science at The University of Queensland, co-advised by Prof. Zhifeng Bao and Dr. Hai Lan.

Research: Abstract reasoning for ARC-AGI-2 and ARC-AGI-3 at Dots. Data agents and memory for the incoming Ph.D. at UQ.

Previously: Research intern in multimodal understanding at Zhipu AI (z.ai) (March–August 2025). Research intern at TSAIL, Tsinghua University (July 2024–September 2025), with Prof. Jun Zhu, Dr. Zehua Chen, and Dr. Duo Su. Research intern at the Center for Future Media, UESTC (May 2023–July 2024), with Prof. Yang Yang and Dr. Jiwei Wei. DevOps with Star Studio, UESTC, from November 2022.

B.Sc. in Computer Science and B.Ec. in Finance, Internet+ Dual Degree, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, June 2025.
